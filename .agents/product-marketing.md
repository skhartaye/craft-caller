# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1
**Last updated:** 2026-11-25
**Status:** Draft grounded in repository and founder requests; pricing not approved.

## Product overview and verified capabilities
Craft Caller is a service-knowledge studio with a local Telegram AI voice prototype. Owners configure services and approved FAQs, practice answers and export snapshots. The local worker uses Faster Whisper, Piper and Ollama; it requires an online Windows PC and a separate regular Telegram recipient account. Local voice playback was heard by the founder, who reported stuttering. Two-way recognition and automatic completion hangup still need live verification. The website cannot initiate calls yet. Email sending, booking and automated follow-up are not implemented.

## Audience and problem hypothesis
Hypothesis: small service business owners want concise, friendly calls that use their approved facts instead of inventing claims. No independent customer interviews, reviews or conversion data are available. The founder requested service-specific rules and answers limited to the question asked. No quantified savings, reliability, sales uplift or customer testimonials are established.

## Offer proposal
Proposed managed pilot, not a published subscription: PHP 1,499 one-time setup for one service and up to ten approved FAQs; optional PHP 499/month support for up to two small script/FAQ updates per month. New features and integrations quoted separately. No unlimited calls, hosting, carrier access, guaranteed response times or cloud provider credits included. Start accepting customers only after live two-way calls and clean hangup are verified. Founder must approve rates and supply contact email before activation. This is a pricing hypothesis, not market research or a validated margin model.

## Voice and messaging
Friendly, clear, short, explicit AI identity, one question at a time. Lead with the recipient's need, answer only what was asked, then check whether it answered their question. Respect refusals. Never claim an email was sent or an appointment made. Do not read the full feature list unsolicited.

## Evidence and learning
Founder report: live voice audible but stuttering, 2026-11-25. Offline speech round trip and bounded FAQ/stop checks pass. This is technical evidence only, not customer success proof. No campaign learnings yet.

## Small future experiment
Hypothesis: a needs-first opener increases qualified requests for a personal demo compared with a feature-first opener. Change only the opening question; keep approved answers, price and contact method identical. Primary outcome: qualified demo requests per consenting completed call; guardrails: opt-outs, unrelated answers and hangup failures. No campaign launched or conversions recorded. Establish baseline and sample plan before claiming a winner.
